# Social Intelligence

## Purpose
The Social Intelligence screen provides a detailed dashboard of social media performance and fan engagement metrics across channels like Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter.

## Metrics on this screen
- `post_match_engagement_rate`
- `reels_engagement_rate`
- `social_total_posts`
- `total_engagement`
- `avg_engagement_per_post`
- `international_engagement_ratio`
- `total_estimated_views`
